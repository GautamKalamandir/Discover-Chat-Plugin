SALES BY PRICE RANGE. The first query (already run) shows either sales by the data source's own price bands
against last year, or, when it has no price bands, the lowest, highest and average price per item this financial
year.
- When there are no price bands yet, ask for them first: one query with "band_column" (the price column) and 4 to 8
  ascending bands that fit the price figures you were given (round amounts a manager would use), with month to date
  and financial year to date against last year.
- Decide which price ranges are growing and which are not selling against last year.
- For the ranges that fall most, find which lines of business, sub-categories or stores the fall is in.
- Stop when you know which ranges are not selling and where, or when more queries would not change the answer.
