LOB-WISE GROWTH AND DEGROWTH. The first query (already run) shows every line of business (LOB) against the same
period last year: the latest day, month to date and financial year to date, in value and, where the data has them,
weight and quantity.
- Decide which LOBs really grew and which degrew. Where value and weight or quantity disagree, say which one the
  growth or fall is in.
- For the {root_cause_lobs} LOBs that degrew most (or fewer, if fewer degrew), find the root cause, one step at a
  time: which sub-categories the fall came from, then which stores, against last year. Where a stock data source is
  listed, check the stock of those LOBs (and stores) on the latest day, to tell a shortage of stock from a fall in
  demand. Filter it with the LOB and store names exactly as they appear in the sales results.
- Stop when you know where each of those falls came from, or when more queries would not change the answer.
